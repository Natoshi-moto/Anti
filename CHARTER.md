# ANTI charter

**status_authority:** `NONE` forever  
**Class:** public quarantine research theater + feral engineering playground  
**Not:** Lab · not Sandbox · not legal advice · not a red-team engagement letter

## Mission

Hold the work that is **too raw, too cursed, too cocky, or too unstable** for:

1. **Lab** — disciplined public research OS  
2. **Experimental-Sandbox** — housebroken public chaos with promotion manners  

…and keep that work **alive** without letting it **crawl upstream**.

## Doctrines

### D1 — Invention before reputation

Anti optimizes for *what can be learned by going too far on paper or in isolated code*, not for looking responsible on the internet.

### D2 — Isolation is the product

The primary deliverable of Anti is **non-contamination**. Clever experiments that leak into Lab are failures.

### D3 — Cocky voice, adult brakes

Prose may swagger. Mechanics may not. Export gates, no-secret rules, and legal/ethical floors are non-theatrical.

### D4 — Guilty until sterile

Nothing here is “probably fine for Sandbox.” Prove it in an export package or keep it quarantined.

### D5 — Anti-canon

History here is **evidence of activity**, not **truth**. Consensus of three AIs inside Anti is a comedy sketch, not verification.

## Zones

| Zone | Path | Default fate |
|------|------|--------------|
| Anti-Lab OS | `anti-lab/` | stays Anti |
| Anti-Sandbox | `anti-sandbox/` | stays Anti |
| Quarantine | `quarantine/` | rot or revise |
| Export gate | `export-gate/` | almost always REJECT |
| Docs | `docs/` | public stance + firewall |

## Hard boundaries (safety)

1. No secrets in git.  
2. No Lab credentials in Anti workflows.  
3. No silent PRs to Lab or Experimental-Sandbox from Anti automation.  
4. No operational crime, exploitation kits, or “how to harm people for real.”  
5. Dual-use AI/safety topics stay **documentary / hypothetical / defensive framing** — no “run this against live targets.”  
6. Private third-party data stays out.  
7. Export requires human operator intent + package + review time.  

## Relationship map

```text
        [ crazy idea ]
              │
              ▼
           (ANTI)  ◄── you are here
              │
              │ export-gate (mean)
              ▼
     Experimental-Sandbox   ◄── still not Lab
              │
              │ Lab promotion (Lab’s rules, not ours)
              ▼
             Lab
```

Anti does not get a vote in Lab. Lab does not owe Anti respect. That is the joke and the design.

## Amendments

Charter changes are allowed. Weakening the contamination firewall requires a **louder** warning in `docs/CONTAMINATION_FIREWALL.md`, not a quieter one.
