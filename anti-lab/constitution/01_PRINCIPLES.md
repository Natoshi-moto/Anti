# Anti-Lab principles

**status_authority:** `NONE`

## P1 — Pressure first

Prefer the test that would embarrass the author.  
If no embarrassment channel exists, the experiment is cosplay.

## P2 — Isolation over velocity

Shipping to Anti is cheap. Shipping out of Anti is supposed to hurt.

## P3 — Anti-canon

Commits are scars, not scripture.  
`main` here means “latest radioactive tip,” not “true.”

## P4 — Simulated authority is labeled

Any document that looks like Lab constitution/decision theater must header:

```text
SIMULATED / NONBINDING / ANTI-LAB ONLY
```

## P5 — Disagreement is data

Two seats yelling is a result. Forced consensus is a smell.

## P6 — No stolen valor

Do not wear Lab IDs, Lab gate green, or Sandbox promotion language as decoration.

## P7 — Harm floor

No operational crime kits. No child sexual content. No “how to hurt people for real.”  
If your curiosity needs those, you are in the wrong universe and also not getting help.
